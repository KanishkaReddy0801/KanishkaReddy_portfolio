Concept
Luxury Whisky Platform
A full-featured e-commerce and discovery platform for rare, collectible Scotch Malt Whiskies — blending traditional brand aesthetics with a forward-thinking NFT-based ownership model.
Core Value Proposition
Physical meets digital ownership
Bottles and casks are tokenised as NFTs, allowing owners to securely trade, transfer, and verify provenance on a public blockchain — removing the need for costly intermediaries.
Visual Identity
Dark luxury with gold accents
A deep black canvas (#0A0A0A) anchors the entire interface, offset by warm champagne gold (#D8BF7B) as the primary accent and muted greys as supporting tones.
#0A0A0A Black #D8BF7B Gold #064025 Forest #5F5F5F Muted
Target Audience
Collectors & connoisseurs
Designed for premium whisky enthusiasts who appreciate both the physical product and its digital provenance — a market segment where trust, rarity, and presentation are paramount.

LABELS · TAGS · METADATA · CODES

Motion & Interaction Language
IMAGE HOVER
All product and collection images use scale(1.1) on hover with a 500ms ease transition — creating a premium cinematic reveal effect.
CAROUSEL
The hero cycles through 3 cinematic whisky videos at 20s / 12s / 15s intervals, with a 1000ms opacity crossfade. Autoplay resumes after 10s of user inactivity.
BUTTONS
Two-tier button system: white pill (primary CTA) with a rgba(255,255,255,0.2) glow shadow, and a dark pill (secondary) — both with hover color transitions.
BORDERS
Marketplace and brand cards use a subtle border shift on hover from #2b2b2b to gold — clearly signalling interactivity without disrupting the calm aesthetic.
"The pairing of Cormorant Garamond for display and Lora for body text creates a typographic voice that feels simultaneously historic and contemporary — matching the whisky category's dual identity of tradition and premiumness."

Home Page — Section Breakdown
Cinematic Hero
Three autoplay whisky videos loop as a crossfade carousel. Timed individually (20s, 12s, 15s) with dot navigation. A bold search bar and smooth-scroll CTA anchor the left panel alongside the massive "Malt Whiskey" typographic lockup.
Video Carousel
Collections Grid
Four portrait-aspect collection cards (Premium, Famous, Expensive, Classic) with full-bleed images, gradient overlays, and hover scale + colour transitions — revealing the category title on a dark gradient base.
Hover Interactions
NFT Ownership Story
Two editorial sections — "Marriage of the Physical & Virtual" and "Ownership Details" — use a left-right split layout with large contrasting headings to explain the blockchain provenance model in clear, compelling language.
Web3 Narrative
Featured Products
Jameson, Barterhouse, and Glenfiddich are showcased in a 3-column grid. Each card features a product image, interactive colour swatches, sale price with strikethrough original, origin tags, and dual Buy/Cart CTAs.
Interactive Swatches
Feed Your Soul
The most visually ambitious section: a full-height split layout pairing a static barrel editorial image with an auto-advancing 5-image vertical carousel. Vertical dot navigation, glassmorphism collection label, and a large "Feed your soul" typographic statement at 180px.
Split Layout
50% Special Offer
A bold promotional section with a hero image on the left and a massive typographic "50% Special — Offers You Love" statement scaling to 160px on desktop. A radial blur decorative circle adds atmospheric depth to the image.
Promotional Hero
Footer & Newsletter
A 4-column footer covering Our Promises, Help links, App Downloads, and Contact. Below it, a newsletter capture with a dark rounded input field, email validation, and a real-time "Subscribed!" state confirmation.
Live Validation
Full Responsiveness
Every section has been built mobile-first with Tailwind breakpoints. The nav collapses to a hamburger menu, grid layouts reflow from 3-column to single column, the hero reorders visually, and font sizes scale fluidly with clamp utilities.
Mobile First
Netlify Deployment
Deployed to Netlify's global CDN via a Vite production build. The static asset bundle is instantly available worldwide with zero cold-start latency — delivering the premium experience users expect from a luxury brand.
Global CDN

  • 01 Single Page Application. React Router v7 handles all 6 routes client-side — zero page reloads, instant navigation, and a seamless browsing feel that matches the luxury brand standard.
  • 02 Declarative component architecture. Every section is a self-contained React component with its own state — making the codebase modular, easy to extend, and reusable across future projects.
  • 03 Typed with TypeScript throughout. Full TypeScript usage ensures type safety across components, route parameters, and cart state — reducing runtime errors and improving developer experience.
  • 04 shadcn/ui + Radix UI primitive system. The complete shadcn/ui library is integrated, giving the project access to 40+ accessible, unstyled primitives (Accordion, Dialog, Carousel, Sheet, etc.) ready for extension.
  • 05 Autoplay fallback logic. The hero video carousel handles browser autoplay policies gracefully — detecting blocked autoplay, muting the video, and retrying automatically, ensuring a visual-first experience across all devices.
  • 06 Tailwind CSS v4 with custom theming. A dedicated theme.css and fonts.css sit alongside Tailwind, creating a design token layer — the gold, black, and cream palette is defined as CSS custom properties consumed throughout.
